# MohFam repository conventions

- External website links must open in a new tab with `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. This includes MohFam division websites and App Store links.
- Keep links within `mohfam.co`, page anchors, email links, and telephone links in their normal browsing context.
- Apply this rule when adding or editing links in templates, shared parts, and JavaScript-generated content.
